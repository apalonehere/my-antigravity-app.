Focus Area 1

Water Conservation Initiative

Addressing climate-induced water scarcity across Barbados.

Barbados is classified as a water-scarce nation. Our youth-led conservation initiative trains participants to identify water leakage, monitor community rainwater harvesting systems, and lead household conservation awareness drives.

Did You Know?

Barbados is in the top 15 most water-stressed countries in the world. Climate change and rising temperatures are making rainfall patterns increasingly erratic.

Focus Area 2

CYEN — Green & Blue Economy Skills

Building youth pathways in sustainable agriculture, renewable energy, and marine stewardship.

Serving Barbadians aged 10-29, the Caribbean Youth Environment Network (CYEN) partnership focuses on practical environmental education, training cohorts in skills that directly support the island's transition to circular and green economies.

Curriculum Matrix

Sustainable Agriculture

Hydroponics, home compost, soil remediation, and organic vegetable production.

Marine Stewardship

Coastal ecosystem mapping, coral monitoring, and ocean literacy.

Climate Advocacy

Public speaking, organizing campaigns, and drafting climate policy recommendations.

Eco-Entrepreneurship

Building eco-friendly small businesses, recycling supply chains, and green branding.

Age-Segmented Pathways

Focuses on basic ecological literacy, environmental games, recycling workshops, and outdoor exploration. Designed to foster early connection to Barbadian nature.

Includes high school workshops, climate action campaigns, hands-on community gardening projects, and certified introductory sustainability courses.

A career transition phase. Provides intensive vocational certifications, internships with marine/environmental organisations, and start-up micro-grants.

Climate Dash & Eco Village

Focused on restoring the local environment. Here, youth plant indigenous trees, install rainwater irrigation, and create organic soil layers to counter erosion.

Focus Area 4

Youth of the Seas (YOTS) Boat Building

Training 10 selected youth per cohort to construct climate-resilient marine vessels.

Targeting young people ages 16-29, particularly SJPI graduates, NEET youth (Not in Education, Employment, or Training), and members of coastal communities. Under expert guidance, this cohort gains hands-on engineering, woodworking, and fiberglass skills.

Curriculum Roadmap

  1. 1 Marine Woodworking & Materials

    Fundamentals of select structural timbers, layout tool handling, and safety protocols.

  2. 2 Disaster-Resilient Construction

    Drafting and reinforcement methods to endure heavy waves and coastal weather events.

  3. 3 Marine Engines & Electronics

    Outboard motor repairs, battery configurations, and basic navigation setup.

  4. 4 Blue Economy Launch

    Marketing vessels, launching cooperative charter ideas, and certification matching.

Cohort Intake Status
