Tomorrow's Reef

They designed a reef before they built one.

Barbados marks 60 years of Independence with a permanent underwater monument, sculpted by young Barbadians, sunk off Carlisle Bay, and left for the coral to finish.

The landmark

Sixty years of Independence, marked underwater rather than on a plinth.

The heritage

Motifs drawn from Barbadian identity: the Broken Trident, coral stone architecture, native marine life.

Where it lands

Carlisle Bay Marine Reserve, in the waters off the Oceana Innovation Hub.

Partners

  • Government of Barbados
  • UNICEF
  • Oceana Innovation Hub
  • Barbados Community College
  • Coastal Zone Management Unit
  • National Conservation Commission
Eco-Leaders Workshop

They told us what the climate space is missing.

A day at the Oceana Innovation Hub with UNICEF Eastern Caribbean. Young people from across Barbados set the agenda; the adults took notes.

Where

The Oceana Innovation Hub: hydroponics under grow lights and a wall built from salvaged bottle caps.

With

UNICEF Eastern Caribbean, alongside Green Rising Barbados.

Who came
