Tomorrow's Reef

Landmark Vision

Overview & Landmark Independence Vision

Toggle Details

As Barbados marks its 60th Anniversary of Independence and 5th year as a Republic in 2026, Tomorrow’s Reef stands as a landmark climate legacy initiative. Led by Green Rising Barbados, this flagship project merges youth leadership, cultural heritage, and marine conservation into a permanent underwater cultural monument.

Designed and fabricated by students and faculty from the Barbados Community College (BCC) Fine Arts Programme, the large-scale sculpture will be deployed off the coast using pH-neutral marine concrete. Over time, it will be colonized by corals and marine life, transforming public art into a living, thriving artificial reef.

Flagship Event

Overview & 3K Frontline Route Vision

Toggle Details

The Climate Dash x Climate Ninja Throwdown is Barbados' premiere youth-led action event. Combining a themed 3K walk/run with interactive kilometre stations and an immersive festival-style Eco-Village, the event unites over 600 students from 21 public and 3 private secondary schools across the island.

Prior to event day, the Green Rising team executes the Climate Ninja Throwdown School Tour across 5–10 secondary schools—hosting high-energy assemblies, fitness warm-ups, and student registration drives that prepare youth for the Usain Bolt Sports Complex finale.
